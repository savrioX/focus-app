How to use Compound

Getting started
Add your top 3 goals — things you're actually working toward this month or semester.
Add 2–5 daily habits. Small, specific, repeatable. "Read 10 pages" not "Read more."
Each morning, write 3 todos — the tasks that move the needle today.
Daily workflow
Check your morning briefing to see Apex's plan for your day.
Log habits by ticking them off each day. Streaks build momentum.
Use the Focus Timer when you need to execute — no distractions.
Cross off todos as you finish. Empty list = great day.
AI features
Apex Advisor — reads your actual data and tells you what's broken and what to do next.
Build habits & tasks — Apex generates a tailored plan from your goals.
Weekly report — see which habits are actually tracking.
Plan my day — Apex writes your todo list from your active goals.
Tips
Set your "What I'm building" context at the top — it helps Apex give better advice.
Habits compound. Show up every day, even when it's inconvenient.
The system only works if you use it daily. 60 seconds is enough.

Questions

Before you sign up.

Is it really free?

Yes — every feature, including the Apex AI coach. No credit card, no trial period, no upsell. AI usage has a fair-use daily cap so one person can't run up the bill for everyone.

Do I have to be a student or a founder?

No. It's built around goals, habits and daily execution, which isn't specific to either. It's shaped for people building something on the side, so that's who it speaks to — but nothing stops you using it for anything else.

What AI powers the Apex Advisor?

Claude, by Anthropic. It reads your actual goals, habits and tasks, so the advice is about your situation rather than generic productivity tips.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It's a web app that works in any mobile browser, and you can add it to your home screen to use it like a native app.

What happens to my data?

It's never sold. AI features send only what's needed to generate a response. Full detail in the Privacy Policy.

Will it stay free?

Everything listed on this page is free today, and there's no paywall in the product. If a paid tier is ever added it'll be for new capability on top — not by taking away what you're already using.
