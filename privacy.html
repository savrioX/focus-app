Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

The short version: We collect only what we need to run the app. We don't sell your data. Your productivity data stays yours.

This Privacy Policy explains how Compound ("we", "us", "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our service.

1. What We Collect

Account information: When you sign up, we collect your username (or Google account name and email if you use Google sign-in).

App data: Your todos, goals, habits, habit logs, and preferences. This is the core data needed to provide the service.

Usage data: Basic technical information like your browser type and device, used to diagnose errors and improve the App.

Payment data: None. Compound is free to use today, there is no paid tier, and we do not collect or process payment information of any kind. If a paid tier is ever added, we will update this policy before collecting any payment data.

Feedback: If you send feedback through the App, we store the message and your user ID.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising
  • We do not read your personal todos or goals for any purpose other than displaying them to you

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the App
  • To sync your data across devices
  • To power AI features (your data is sent to Anthropic's API when you use AI features — see below)
  • To respond to feedback and support requests

4. AI Features and Anthropic

When you use AI features (Apex Advisor, daily planning, habit feedback, etc.), summaries of your goals, habits, and todos are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses. This data is processed by Anthropic under their Privacy Policy.

We send only what's necessary for the AI to respond helpfully. We do not store your AI conversations beyond your current session.

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database platform. Data is stored in the US. Supabase employs industry-standard security practices including encryption at rest and in transit.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your data (contact us and we'll provide it)

To exercise these rights, email us at hello@dailycompound.app.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage to cache your data for faster loading and to remember your preferences (dark mode, font size, API key if provided). We do not use advertising cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

Compound is intended for users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us at hello@dailycompound.app and we will delete it promptly.

10. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — AI features
  • Vercel — hosting and serverless functions

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@dailycompound.app
