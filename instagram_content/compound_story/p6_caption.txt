Building the app was the easy part.

I'm 19. I built a productivity SaaS called Compound from scratch and launched it. The hard part wasn't the code.

The hard part:
- Figuring out business structure before your first dollar
- Understanding what a Terms of Service actually needs to say
- Learning that chargebacks can destroy you if your refund policy is vague
- Realising you need a lawyer before you need customers

Nobody teaches you this stuff. You figure it out alone, at midnight, when it's already too late.
