GENERAL TIPS:
• On Instagram Reels, trending audio gets pushed by the algorithm — always check "Trending" tab when posting
• For carousels/single images, music is optional but adds watch time
• Lofi/chill beats = safe choice for any chapter when nothing trending fits
• Keep volume at 60–70% so voiceover or text can be read in silence too
• TikTok sounds often cross to Reels — check both platforms for what's trending that week
