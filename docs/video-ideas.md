### VIDEO 2: YCombinator Free Agents

**Pillar:** War Stories / Hot Take
**Hook:** "YC just released their free agents list. Here's what that means for me at 19."
**Format:** Talking head, direct to camera. Show the YC list on screen briefly.
**Context:** YC's free agents program lists talented people (often recent grads or paused students) who want to join early-stage startups. It's a signal of the current talent market.

**Researched Hook Variants (pick one):**
1. "YCombinator just quietly changed the game and nobody explained it right." *(contrarian + urgency)*
2. "YC isn't funding apps anymore. They're funding AI that replaces people. Here's what that means." *(bold claim)*
3. "The YC batch that changes everything: free agents are here." *(timeliness + open loop)*
4. "I'm a 19-year-old building an app. YC just announced they want to fund what I'm doing. Here's why." *(personal stakes)*
5. **"Hot take: YC's 'free agents' trend means solo founders like me are the new YC company."** *(RECOMMENDED — uniquely yours)*

**Optimal length: 18-30 seconds** — hot takes live or die fast, land it and leave

**Script:**

```
[HOOK — 0:00-0:03]
"YCombinator just dropped their free agents list.
And it made me rethink everything about building solo."

[SETUP — 0:03-0:15]
"YC free agents — for context — are talented people who've paused careers

Not the other way around.

design, code, growth — DM me.
I don't have funding but I have momentum and a story people are watching."

"Day [X]. Building."
```
