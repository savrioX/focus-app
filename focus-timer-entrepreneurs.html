Why entrepreneurs need a different kind of focus timer

The Pomodoro technique works. The problem for entrepreneurs and founders is context: you might be context-switching between coding, writing, outreach, and strategy in the same day. A simple timer doesn't help you track which type of work you're doing or whether it's moving the right needle.

Compound's focus timer lives alongside your goal and task system. You can see what you're supposed to be working on, start a session, and come back to check off the task when the session ends — all in one screen.

How the focus timer works

  • Pomodoro-style sessions — 25-minute focus blocks with 5-minute breaks by default, adjustable to fit your style
  • Built into your todo list — start a session directly while looking at your task list, no tab switching
  • Ambient audio — optional focus sounds to mask distractions during deep work sessions
  • No interruptions by design — the timer is simple on purpose; the complexity lives in the goal and habit system beside it

The real productivity bottleneck for founders isn't time — it's context
