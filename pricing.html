Common questions

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from account settings at any time. Pro access continues until end of billing period. No questions asked.
What AI powers Apex Advisor?
Apex Advisor is powered by Claude by Anthropic — one of the most capable AI models available. It knows your goals, habits, and tasks to give coaching that is actually relevant to your life.
Is my data private?
Yes. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. When you use AI features, only what is needed is sent to Anthropic to generate a response. See our Privacy Policy.
