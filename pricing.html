Common questions

When does Pro launch?
Very soon. Join the waitlist inside the app (click any Pro feature) — you'll be emailed the moment it goes live. Follow @thestartupjournal1 for the live drop.
Monthly or annual — what's the difference?
Annual billing is $96/year ($8/month equivalent) — you save $24 vs paying monthly. Monthly is $10/month, no commitment. Both cancel anytime.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, anytime. No contracts. Your free tier features stay active after you cancel.
What AI powers Apex Advisor?
Apex is powered by Claude by Anthropic — one of the most capable AI models available. It reads your actual goals, habits, and tasks to give advice that's relevant to your life, not generic templates.
Is my data private?
Yes. We never sell your data. AI features only send what's needed to generate a response. See our Privacy Policy.
How is payment handled?
All payments go through Stripe. We never store card details. Manage or cancel from account settings anytime.
